🛠️ The Story of Marble Blast Mark Edition

  1. Project Launch: Marble Blast Engels
    It all began with the development of a mod called Marble Blast Engels. This phase established the mod’s foundation, introduced visual changes, and set the groundwork for future iterations.
  2. A Hiatus and a Major Rebrand (Version 0.04)
    Following a break in development, the project returned at full throttle with significant updates. The mod shed its old title and was officially rebranded as Marble Blast Mark Edition. The build introduced a level titled "PowerDive!", centered around the power-ups.
  3. Hitting the Forums and a Fateful Response
    In September 2026, Mark launched a thread on the official Marble Blast forums. The mod immediately caught the eye of KS—an experienced speedrunner and creator—who jumped into the discussion with words of praise. He expressed interest in speedrunning the mod and asked "and also. can i port this to speedrun edition?" Although a forum admin chimed in with a dismissive comment "Sir, this is just MBG reskinned without any original levels." Mark simply ignored them. Instead, he gave a clear, confident "Yes" to KS, setting the stage for a brand-new version!
  4. Creating the Speedrun Edition Port
    With the green light given, the project officially expanded. An official port for Speedrun Edition was developed, bringing all the engaging, fun-filled gameplay—including the laid-back "PowerDive!" level—to a new platform for fans of fast-paced, dynamic action!
